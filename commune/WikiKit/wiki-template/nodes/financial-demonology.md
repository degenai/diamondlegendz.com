# financial-demonology

The field [[Alex]] founded and named (July 2026): **Shannon's Demon expanded from a curiosity into a full lens for portfolio management** — value studied in the spreads *between* assets rather than the assets themselves. The lineage runs Maxwell's demon → Shannon → Fernholz's stochastic portfolio theory: the *scientific* demonology, not the occult wealth-magick squatting on the search term.

## The frame
- **Both clauses load-bearing:** the interesting object is the *process* — what the trades between assets do — studied on things you could hold forever anyway.
- **The rent theorem (Lesson Zero):** renting returns means the lessor earns your return *plus* your rent; the only move is owning the process. Hence the field is free — doctrine, data, games, and code all published.
- **Robin Hood framing:** the structure favors small operators (it's capacity-protected), and the apparatus that's normally class-gated is exactly what gets redistributed. Guardrail: promise ownership, never riches.

## The meteorology era (July 2026) — the field's biggest fold, and its rebirth
The campaign **folded on its central claim.** The excess growth rate turned out not to be free harvestable yield — buy-and-hold captures almost all of it, and two peer-reviewed papers plus the lab's own simulator agreed to four decimals. So the ladder, in [[Alex]]'s words: **demon SELECTION — dead; demon PREDICTION (picking winners) — dead physics; DEMON METEOROLOGY — alive: "predicting textures, not specifics"** (dispersion, co-movement, divorce hazard — the estimable family; never direction). An evolutionary tournament of invented sensors (the Royal Rumble) and a corpse library of dead tickers (the Hall of the Dead) now train failure-detectors instead of winner-pickers. **All of it is public, including the fold** — {Alex}'s ruling: *"the reason I don't get rich isn't gonna be because I published this."* The retraction was the victory: the field now knows what it hunts — a barometer, not a stock-picker.
