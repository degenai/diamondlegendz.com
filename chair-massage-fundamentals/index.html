Chair Massage Fundamentals

Module 1: Pressure

This module needs a keyboard and a mouse.

Pressure is taught on W and S and the stroke guide follows the mouse, so a phone or tablet cannot take Module 1. Please come back on a desktop or laptop computer. Thank you for your patience.

Made by The People's Elbow, a.k.a. Alex Adamczyk, LMT.

The real chair is at The People's Elbow, Woodstock, GA. peoples-elbow.com

Learning objectives

  • Apply appropriate pressure on the stroke ring (W / S).
  • Select the modality the client requests (A / D).
  • Track stroke patterns with the mouse on the guide.
  • Transition efficiently between clients (E).

Controls: W / S pressure, A / D modality, mouse on the guide, E next client.

Format: Interactive
Duration: approx. 5 min
Prerequisites: None
Assessment: None
Instructor: Alex Adamczyk, LMT
