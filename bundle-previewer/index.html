What is this? Bulk Graph Bundler (BBL) is a curation project that pulls trading-card singles from bulk inventory the rest of the market sells by weight, sorts them by theme, and ships them as one-off Discrete Lairs. Each lair is a numbered SKU. Numbers append-only; never reused. This page is a sandbox preview. The commercial storefront will live on a different URL.
