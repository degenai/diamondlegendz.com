# Alex

Friend and collaborator of [[Andy]]; co-owner of [[diamondlegendz|Diamond Legendz]]. Licensed massage therapist (LMT) in Woodstock, Georgia, and a self-taught coder who builds his own tools instead of renting them.

This is the friendly-collaborator sketch — the public-facing Alex that shows up in the shared work. His own wiki (alexpedia) holds the rest.

## What he does
- **Massage therapist** — LMT in Woodstock. Runs **the People's Elbow**, a mutual-aid chair-massage outfit with superhero/wrestler branding: he works events and splits the take 50/50 with the host venue, who aims their half at a cause. "Licensed hands. Half the money stays on your block."
- **Builder** — self-taught; writes his own CRMs, dashboards, and research engines. Treats projects as *experiments, not theses* — ships, measures, retracts when the data says so.
- **Founder of [[financial-demonology|Financial Demonology]]** — the study of volatility harvesting as a full portfolio lens, run out of his [[DemonRanchKit|Demon Ranch]] research lab. A public field, free by design.

- "Get paid for time, not risk." The means of production is his own hands.
