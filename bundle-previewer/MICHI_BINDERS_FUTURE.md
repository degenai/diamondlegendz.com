# Future feature: Michi method binders

The Michi method (originated by @michimaybe_ / ig: peeplop in the Pokemon TCG community) is a binder-page layout approach where a 9-slot page holds 5-7 actual cards and uses the remaining 2-4 slots for intentional non-card art inserts — extended art panels, character cutouts, sleeve-back texture, or title cards — that make the curatorial thesis visible rather than leaving it implicit in the card selection alone. For BBL, this maps directly to the narrative-first / museum-curation doctrine: the art inserts ARE the wall text; the cards are the artifacts on display.

The storefront application would be a new render target alongside the existing card carousel — either an HTML binder-page view, a printable PDF digital deliverable, or both. The BBL graph already supplies the data: `art_crop_url` fields for insert candidates, character/symbol/hub nodes for thesis grounders, `cohesion.palette_hex` for backgrounds, and the bundle `narrative` for the title-card insert text.
