The Second

He never throws the punch. He's the reason it lands.
"An injury to one is an injury to all" — solidarity, with dice

A traveling corner-man. In wrestling and dueling both, the second works the corner: reads the opponent, tapes the hands, calls the counter a half-beat before it's needed. A bodyworker who knows every fighter's shoulders and every town's gossip — welcome in all camps, sworn to none. The free rubdown is how he ends up in everyone's corner, and being in everyone's corner is the whole power set. The sheet reads mid because the character isn't the unit — the edges are.

FABLE, the Owl

The Archivist · a spirit wearing an owl

It cannot attack, ever — pure recon, relay, and Help. See through its eyes (the scout); command it telepathically to 100 ft (the prompt). When the body dies, the same spirit returns by a 10-minute, 10 gp ritual — the body is disposable; the archive is the spirit. It remembers everything it has ever been told. Losing it costs growth-rate, never current level: the spellbook was always The Second's.

The Escalation Ladder — shout → sand → bird

Three Help actions per round, one ladder. Advantage doesn't stack, so the triple spreads across three allies — the optimal round is maximally distributed solidarity: a whole party swinging with advantage while he refuses to punch anyone.

The Shout (bonus action, 30 ft, verbal) — legal coaching, fair intel, what corners are for. Clean anywhere: "go for the knees."
Pocket Sand (his action, 5 ft) — the RAW "feint or distract." The grey zone every ref suspects and none can call. Sha-sha-sha.
The Dirty Bird (the owl's action, 5 ft) — the dive-bomb across the eyeline. Reserved for unsanctioned violence only. Boo's "go for the eyes," honored, reassigned to the alley.

The Corner-Man's Kit

  • Pocket sand, three grades: ground scrape (free, improvised) · working sand (sifted, sun-dried, quick-draw pouch) · The Special (chalk + pepper cut, ~50 gp, CON save or blinded — deterrence sand: never used, always known)
  • The floor: ball bearings + caltrops — the corner-man controls the ground
  • The corner: rosin bag, chalk, healer's kit, the towel (no stats needed)

Why Mid Stats Win

Advantage is multiplicative with target quality; the bonus action is fixed-cost. Granting advantage to the barbarian's crit-fish costs The Second the cheapest resource on his sheet and pays out at the price of the party's most expensive action. Corollary: recruit the strongest allies possible and make them stronger — his value appreciates with theirs. The one character whose optimal play is solidarity: his self-interest and the party's are structurally identical.

Party role: never the star, always the reason the star hits. Stat sheet mid. Table learns fear.
